For the owner to decide

  1. Constraint keys. Fusion gives constraints no letters. The design keeps Locus's letters where they don't clash (H, K, E, F, Q, W, Y) and moves the clashing ones to Shift (Shift+T tangent, Shift+O concentric, Shift+M midpoint, Shift+X collinear). Alternative: no letters, like Fusion.
  2. Horizontal/Vertical. Fusion has one combined tool that picks by the line's angle. The design follows Fusion (H); V goes away.
  3. Style on Cut and Score layers. Objects carry their own style, but LightBurn and Cricut read colour as the operation. The design exports Cut/Score/Drag-knife strokes in the profile's colour and hairline, and shows that in the Style section. Draw and Engrave export the object's own style.
  4. Marking-menu ring. The 8 ring slots stay the same everywhere so flicks become muscle memory; only the list beneath changes with context.
  5. Style for new geometry. New geometry takes the "current style" shown in the Inspector when nothing is selected. Picking with the eyedropper with nothing selected sets the current style.

Not in this mockup

Machine preview, validation, plugins, MCP and touch keep their current behaviour and move into the new chrome unchanged. Touch long-press opens the same marking menu.
